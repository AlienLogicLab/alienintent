## Mechanical completeness (not exhaustive ambiguity detection)

Inspection reads only the labelled fields in each current requirement's normalized
definition body: `Intent:`, `Scope:`, `Non-goals:`, `Authority:`, `Acceptance:`. Items are
`;`-separated. Rule codes:

- `MISSING_INTENT`: the Intent field is absent or empty.
- `MISSING_SCOPE`: the Scope field is absent or empty.
- `SCOPE_CONFLICT`: an item appears (case-insensitively) in both Scope and Non-goals.
- `MISSING_AUTHORITY`: the Authority field is absent or empty, or the source
  authority status is not approved.
- `UNDEFINED_ACCEPTANCE`: the Acceptance field is absent, or any item lacks an
  `<id>: <result>` measurable result, or the result is a placeholder
  (TBD/TODO/undefined/unknown/n/a/?).
- `AMBIGUOUS_DEPENDENCY`: a dependency is unresolved, conflicting or retired in the snapshot.
- `CONFLICTING_SOURCES`: U1 reports the requirement ID as a conflict (differing
  definitions). A single finding covers every conflicting source span.

Identifier or source issues in U1 that overlap the requirement's spans make its
mechanical status `UNVERIFIED` (a hold), never complete. A retired requirement is not
preparable.

Identity: finding = SHA-256(canonical[project, requirement revision, rule code, sorted
source locators]); a semantic finding also includes the immutable reviewer finding
reference. Resolution = SHA-256(canonical[finding, decision idempotency key, actor]). For
conflicts, the "revision" is the sorted set of conflicting revisions.

## Questions, decisions and holds

Every OPEN finding records its source locators, a question naming the affected
requirement, a blocked advancement reason, the required decision actor (configured
by composition, never taken from source text or review text), and the affected
requirement plus its transitive dependents. When a decision channel is configured,
each question is registered in the existing control_plane `DecisionInbox` as
`HumanDecisionRequired(work_item="upstream-question:<finding_id>")` with options
`resolve`/`defer`. The inbox's admission for these questions is
`UpstreamQuestionAdmission`. It validates the actor and never resumes or launches work.
With no channel, the finding stays durably OPEN.

`resolve(finding_id, DecisionRecord, input_revision, expected_version)` returns
`Resolution` or `Hold`. The outcomes are:

- `STALE_INPUT`: the expected version conflicts. There is no blind retry.
- `STALE_INSPECTION`: the inventory changed after the last inspection.
- `UNKNOWN_FINDING`: no finding has that ID.
- `REVISION_HOLD`: the finding is STALE, or `input_revision` differs from the finding's
  pinned revision.
- `FINDING_MISMATCH`: the decision names another question.
- `AUTHORITY_HOLD`: the actor is not the required actor, the event and submission
  actors differ, or the decision is not durably recorded in the DecisionInbox for this
  question.
- `DECISION_NOT_RESOLVE`: the choice is not `resolve`.
- A matching decision appends RESOLVED to that finding only.

A rejected answer is appended as retained evidence, but the finding stays OPEN.
Re-inspecting against a changed requirement revision appends STALE to its prior
OPEN or RESOLVED findings. The new revision gets fresh findings, and prior resolutions
do not carry over. An unchanged requirement keeps its finding identities and statuses,
so replay is idempotent.

A requirement's preparation is `ELIGIBLE_FOR_PREPARATION` only when U1 resolves it as
eligible, mechanical status is COMPLETE or all mechanical findings are RESOLVED, no
semantic finding is OPEN or STALE-unreplaced, the semantic review input names its
current revision, and no dependency is HELD. Otherwise the requirement is `HELD`, with
finding IDs and reasons. Holds apply to the affected branch only.

Semantic review: attributable `SemanticReview` input (reviewer, immutable review
reference, requirement ID and revision, questions with locators) is imported as
findings. Its semantic review status is reported separately from mechanical
completeness. The requirement's revision and semantic text stay byte-identical in the
report, so no intent is rewritten. A review naming a non-current revision makes the
requirement `UNVERIFIED` (held). No model is invoked.

## Persistence

Aggregate `upstream:ambiguity:current` in the injected `OperationalStore`
(`SQLiteOperationalStore`) holds `schema_version: 1`, the inventory digest, the report
ref/digest, and findings with append-only status history. The report, resolution and
rejected-answer bodies are immutable `Observation`s in the injected
`EvidenceRepository` (access label `private`). Each is installed and read back before a
CAS commit with expected version. An unknown schema version holds without mutation.
