## Behavior (pinned before implementation)

New modules live in `context_assembly`, which is the contract's implementation location. The
premise, architecture and authority adapters live in `composition`, the outer boundary. No new
Project lifecycle state, service, database or approval lane is added. Internal states are
`MECHANICALLY_HELD`, `REVIEW_REQUIRED`, `VERIFIED` and `STALE`, the SWF-25 semantic gate
states. They are persisted only in `upstream:design:<design_key>` aggregates.

- **Domain `context_assembly/domain/design_admission.py`.**
  - `DesignContract` is parsed from a canonical JSON document with these parts:
    - `design_key` (the requirement-set key);
    - `requirements` (requirement ID → revision digest);
    - `producer` (actor, invocation);
    - `fields` (the eleven AC-01 fields, each either `{"value": [...]}` or `{"inapplicable": "<reason>"}`);
    - `decisions` (`id`, `category` ∈ BEHAVIOR/PUBLIC_API/PERSISTENCE/SECURITY/IMPLEMENTATION_LOCAL, `status` ∈ FIXED/OPEN, `statement`, `bound`);
    - `interface_manifest` (`port`, `producer`, `consumer`, `provides`, `requires`);
    - `premises` (`premise_id`, `criterion`, `requested`);
    - `dependency_edges` (`source`, `target`).

    Its digest is the sha256 of the canonical document.
  - The AC-01 fields are:
    - `satisfied_requirements`
    - `affected_behavior`
    - `ownership`
    - `interfaces`
    - `persistence`
    - `invariants`
    - `failures_recovery`
    - `security`
    - `capabilities`
    - `evidence`
    - `non_goals`
  - `inspect_design(design, architecture, authority, premises) -> MechanicalReport`. It runs the checks in the order below. Every check runs and reports, and the status is `MECHANICALLY_HELD` if any check holds, otherwise `REVIEW_REQUIRED`.
  - `admit_review(design, report, review, reviewers) -> ReviewAdmitted | ReviewRefused`.
  - `applicability(state, current_vector) -> CurrentVerified | Held | Stale`.
- **Port `context_assembly/ports/design_admission.py`.**
  - `ArchitectureChecks.run() -> ArchitectureReport` returns the named existing check results and the baseline digest.
  - `PremiseCheck.check(premise_id, requested) -> PremiseResult`.
  - `DirectionAuthoritySource.read() -> DirectionAuthority` returns the gap id, status, resolution actor, source ref and descriptive `observed_edges`.
- **Application `context_assembly/application/design_admission_service.py`.**
  - `DesignAdmission` provides `inspect(document, expected_version)`,
    `record_review(design_key, review, expected_version)`,
    `check(design_key, current_vector)` (the `DesignApplicability.check` port),
    `history(design_key)` and `retained(ref)`.
  - `DesignReadiness.admit(design_key, current_vector) -> ReadinessDecision` is the readiness-processing gate. It admits only `CurrentVerified`.
- **Composition `composition/design_admission.py`.**
  - `RepositoryArchitectureChecks(checker, source_root)` runs the unchanged `tools/fitness/check_architecture.py` once per existing check.
  - `PremiseReaderCheck(reader)` adapts the U3 reader.
  - `RetainedDirectionAuthority(root, path, sha256, gap_id, project, profile)` reads the pinned `authority_gaps` entry and the descriptive graph.
  - `UpstreamProfile` gains the optional `design_checks`, `design_authority` and `design_reviewers`. It exposes `profile.design` and `profile.design_readiness`, and the existing constructor stays compatible.

### `inspect_design` checks, in this order

1. `existing-architecture`:
   - no `ArchitectureChecks` gives `ARCHITECTURE_BASELINE_MISSING`;
   - a missing one of `layering`, `vendor-signature`, `port-contract`, `configuration` or `determinism` gives `ARCHITECTURE_CHECK_UNAVAILABLE`;
   - any failing check gives `ARCHITECTURE_CHECK_FAILED`, naming the violations.
2. `interface-compatibility`: a consumer `requires` entry not in the producer's `provides` for the same port gives `INCOMPATIBLE_INTERFACE`.
3. `contract-completeness`:
   - an absent AC-01 field gives `INCOMPLETE_DESIGN`;
   - an inapplicable field without a nonblank reason gives `UNEXPLAINED_INAPPLICABILITY`;
   - an inapplicable `satisfied_requirements`, or a `satisfied_requirements` value unequal to the `requirements` keys, gives `REQUIREMENT_LINK_MISMATCH`.
4. `decisions`:
   - an OPEN material decision gives `UNRESOLVED_MATERIAL_DECISION` (return to SPECIFY);
   - an OPEN `IMPLEMENTATION_LOCAL` choice without a nonblank `bound` gives `UNBOUNDED_LOCAL_CHOICE`;
   - a bounded local choice stays open.
5. `platform-premise`:
   - each premise is read through `PremiseCheck` with its own requested observables;
   - an infeasible result gives `INFEASIBLE_PREMISE`, carrying the U3 reason, the missing observables and the evidence refs;
   - premises with no `PremiseCheck` give `PREMISE_EVIDENCE_UNAVAILABLE`.

   The design's own claims are never read as satisfiability.
6. `direction-authority`: any declared `dependency_edges` entry makes admission direction-dependent.
   - If the gap is not disposed by its resolution actor, the result is `ARCHITECTURE_AUTHORITY_HOLD`. This includes an unavailable authority.
   - If it is disposed, the result is `DIRECTION_SCOPE_UNREVIEWED`, because no reviewed scoped policy exists in this BIU.

   The check never passes a declared edge, and `observed_edges` is never consulted as policy.

The report records:

- the revision vector:
  - `design`: the design digest;
  - `requirements`: the map;
  - `architecture`: the baseline digest;
  - `premises`: the digest of the premise results;
  - `authority`: the digest of the authority snapshot;
- the ordered check results;
- the holds.

### `admit_review` rules, in this order

1. `INVALID_REVIEW`: the record is malformed, or a REJECTED review has no finding.
2. `NO_MECHANICAL_REPORT`: no mechanical report exists yet, so review cannot precede the checks.
3. `STALE_REVIEW`: the reviewed design digest, report digest or revision vector differs from the current ones.
4. `PRODUCER_MISMATCH`: the review's producer is not the design's recorded producer.
5. `SELF_REVIEW`: the reviewer actor equals the producer actor, or the reviewer invocation equals the producer invocation.
6. `UNAUTHORIZED_REVIEWER`: the reviewer actor is not configured reviewer authority.
7. `MECHANICAL_HOLD_OUTSTANDING`: a VERIFIED decision on a `MECHANICALLY_HELD` report.
8. `BLOCKING_FINDINGS`: a VERIFIED decision with any blocking finding.

Otherwise a VERIFIED review makes the design `VERIFIED` and binds its vector. A REJECTED review
is retained, and the design stays `REVIEW_REQUIRED` with the reason `REVIEW_REJECTED`. A refused
review is retained as `design.review_refused` and changes no applicability. An identical
admitted review replayed at the same revision is a no-op.

### Applicability and invalidation

`check` returns:

- `CurrentVerified` only for `VERIFIED` with an identical vector;
- `Stale` for a changed vector key, naming the changed keys. A `design.stale` event is committed, so it stays stale until it is re-inspected and reviewed afresh;
- `Held` with `NO_DESIGN`, `MECHANICALLY_HELD` (with its holds), `REVIEW_REQUIRED` or `REVIEW_REJECTED` otherwise.

A new design revision appends a new `inspected` event. Every design, report, review and stale
record is an immutable Observation, chained by `preceding_refs` to the event before it. The
history is append-only and has exactly one event per committed version, so prior artifacts and
verdicts stay retrievable. Events are exposed as `design.mechanical_held`,
`design.review_required`, `design.verified`, `design.stale`, `design.review_rejected` and
`design.review_refused`. No Project `DESIGN` status or transition is emitted.

retained premise reader, the pinned authority record and the real architecture checker over

  `INCOMPLETE_DESIGN`, `persistence` inapplicable with a blank reason gives

- **AC-02.** An OPEN `PUBLIC_API` decision gives `UNRESOLVED_MATERIAL_DECISION`. An unbounded
  local choice gives `UNBOUNDED_LOCAL_CHOICE`, and a bounded one is admitted. A VERIFIED review with a
  blocking finding gives `BLOCKING_FINDINGS`.

  realizable observables) gives `INFEASIBLE_PREMISE` with the U3 `UNACHIEVABLE_PREMISE`. A VERIFIED
  review of it gives `MECHANICAL_HOLD_OUTSTANDING`. A REJECTED review is retained. The four
  realizable observables alone are feasible.
- **AC-05.** After VERIFIED, a changed requirement revision gives `Stale(["requirements"])`, and a
  new design revision gives `Held(REVIEW_REQUIRED)` while the old vector gives `Stale`. The prior
  VERIFIED review record remains readable.
- **AC-06.** Readiness refuses `NO_DESIGN`, `REVIEW_REQUIRED` and a direction-dependent design
  (`MECHANICALLY_HELD` with `ARCHITECTURE_AUTHORITY_HOLD`). Only `upstream:design:` aggregates exist
  in the store, and `LifecycleStage` is exactly IMPLEMENT, VERIFY, REVIEW, ACCEPT, DONE.

  - The report lists all five existing checks, PASS over `src/alienintent`.
  - Over `tests/fixtures/fitness/layering`, the result is `ARCHITECTURE_CHECK_FAILED` naming `domain imports adapters`.
  - A consumer requiring an unprovided input gives `INCOMPATIBLE_INTERFACE`.
  - The edge `execution_coordination -> installation`, which *is* in `observed_edges`, still gives `ARCHITECTURE_AUTHORITY_HOLD`.
- **051-review-applicability.**
  - Self-review, a shared invocation, a replayed stale review, an unauthorized reviewer and review-before-checks are each refused, and readiness is refused afterwards.
  - The conforming independent review is admitted, and readiness admits.
