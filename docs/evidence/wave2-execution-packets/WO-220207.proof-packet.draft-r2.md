### What FX-U7 proves, and what it must not claim (U7 / U8 boundary)

FX-U7 proves **validation of candidates** and nothing else: pure checks over supplied candidate values, typed holds that name the offending element, a persisted validation report or hold, and the absence of any lifecycle, release, projection or decomposition write.

It applies to two candidate modes (`C#/contracts/2/state`: "mode INITIAL or SPLIT_REPLAN"):
- **INITIAL.** A hand-authored candidate obligation mapping (`C#/contracts/2/ports/1`, `/ports/2`). Success is "admitted for assessment" and proves validation only. It does **not** prove that U7 or any other unit derived that mapping.
- **SPLIT_REPLAN.** A frozen split proposal, meaning the value `SplitTransaction.prepare` would return (`C#/contracts/2/ports/3`, `/state`, `/transitions/3`). FX-U7 validates it: forward and reverse conservation, bounds, authority binding, graph identity rewrites, identity bounds, lineage and the stale-assessment invalidation set. Success is "admitted for assessment" and proves validation only.

FX-U7 must **not** claim any of the following:
- initial derivation (`InitialCompilation.compile`, `C#/contracts/2/ports/0`, `/transitions/0`). This is U8's `D#/nodes/10/completion_predicate`, and `B#/bius/9` itself says "U8 must derive initial decomposition";
- `SplitTransaction.prepare`/`apply`, exact-payload authorization issuance, identity *reservation/allocation*, the canonical decomposition aggregate commit, atomic apply/readback, recovery, invalidation *records* or materialization of results (`C#/contracts/2/persistence`, `/concurrency`, `/recovery`). These are apply/enforcement, in U8's predicate;
- lint, submission or reassessment of results through SF-REQ-015 (`C#/contracts/2/transitions/4`);
- `013-graph-coverage` (`D#/enforcement_obligations/2`, owner U8).

**DAG gap (recorded, not resolved).** The re-specification appended SF-REQ-013-AC-06, AC-07 and AC-08 (`S#/candidates/2/acceptance_criteria/5..7`; `C#/contracts/2/acceptance_design_trace/5..7`). `D#/acceptance_trace` has no row for them. The U7 predicate's "deterministic identity rewrites, preserved predicates, lineage and immutable stale-assessment history" overlaps AC-07/AC-08 text, and U8's predicate also names them. The repair record says this is "a DAG trace change" to be settled "in a DAG revision before U8 or A is scheduled".

FX-U7 therefore works as follows:
- It derives its identity-rewrite, lineage and assessment-history probes from the **U7 completion predicate** and from `C#/contracts/2/design_decisions/4`, `/identities`, `/failure_modes`, `/security`, `/evidence`.
- `run-report.json` maps those probes to the predicate clause `U7-PREDICATE` only, **never** to SF-REQ-013-AC-06, -AC-07 or -AC-08. It records `ac_06_08_trace_ownership: UNASSIGNED_IN_DAG`.
