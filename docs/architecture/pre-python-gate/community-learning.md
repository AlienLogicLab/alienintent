## Contribution boundary
A contribution is a newly derived schema-validated object, never a private record marked upload=true. It contains task class, provider/model/capability versions, measurement definitions, generalized failure/intervention/outcome and qualified cost/quality provenance. No proprietary source, credentials, private execution content or hidden reasoning is required. Consent binds destination, permitted fields, purpose and policy version; queued exports must re-check consent before sending. Local evidence and community retention/compaction are separately governed.

Receiving/query adapters validate schema, provenance, duplication, sanitization and poisoning indicators. Evidence credibility is not contributor popularity. Access does not require a full-corpus download or a contribution competition. Retain measurements and uncertainty; shared correlation never becomes automatic high-impact policy authority. Human-approved proposals/experiments mediate learned policy changes.

## Scenarios
OFF produces no contribution traffic. Revocation before send cancels queued export. A field containing a secret or source snippet is rejected, not blindly trusted because client marked it sanitized. A malicious high-performance claim cannot change deployment capabilities. Public baseline evidence may be queried through an explicitly configured adapter without requiring private contributions.

Older Cloudflare-first service deployment remains a portability example, not a core hosting requirement. Destination selection and irreversible public sharing remain deployment/Founder authority; this design sends nothing and grants no export consent.
