## Intent
AlienIntent turns product intent into bounded, authorized engineering work, coordinates interchangeable workers, verifies outcomes and completes operational obligations while preserving usable engineering evidence. Its users are self-hosting operators, engineering teams and agents acting under their authority. Success means accepted useful outcomes with fewer interventions and attributable cost, not more tasks or tokens.

Work can begin without a repository. A Work Item may lead to multiple BIUs over a repository DAG; no cross-repository atomic Git transaction is promised. The product owns execution coordination and readiness integration, not a second implementation of upstream specification/decomposition or Agent-Ready. Spec Kit is an optional upstream adapter, not a required product dependency.

Work-unit DONE proves its bounded obligations, not whole-feature convergence, business success, public-release readiness or Python Sovereignty. The learning loop distinguishes observed improvement, correlation and causal evidence. Self-hosting is canonical; no ALL-hosted service is required.
